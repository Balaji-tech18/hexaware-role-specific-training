On Premise Computing needs so much work ,
for example let we say the big billion days is coming and there is lot of traffic for 2 months so hexaware bought two on premise servers to maintain the traffic and load.
Problems or Work Hexaware had to face :
->need approval from CTO , Directors etc.,
->Buying and setup of server takes 2-3 weeks of time 
->need network engineers for 24/7 , so 3 teams and 1 backup team need which costs investment
->need to pay rent for the server room.
->power supply needed for 24/7

Then comes the real problem , after the 2 month the traffic is back to normal and the use of the newly bought servers is no more needed which  results in poor ROI.

That is why Cloud Computing is more profitable and useful because ,
->It is scalable we can buy as much servers we need for a particular time and then stop paying for it when the use is completed.
->It is elastic , we can increase and decrease the servers or services we want as per need. 
->no need of physical server room or IT team to maintain it .
